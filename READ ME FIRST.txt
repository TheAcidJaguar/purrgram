Purrgram 🐱  — a little cat that lives on your Mac desktop

INSTALL
 1. Open the download, then drag Purrgram onto the Applications folder
    shortcut right next to it (or into Applications in any Finder window).
 2. Double-click it. macOS will probably say it "can't be opened"
    because it isn't from the App Store. To allow it:
      • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security
      • Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Purrgram
      • Click Open again when asked
    (On older macOS you can instead right-click the app → Open → Open.)
    Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Purrgram.app"
 3. Name your cat!

PLAYING
 • Click the cat ............ pet (they purr — but don't overdo it)
 • Double-click the cat ..... shoo them out of the way
 • Drag the cat ............. pick them up (drop them on a window!)
 • Click the bowls .......... fill food / water (bottom-right corner)
 • Cat face in the menu bar .. stats, treats, yarn, mice, brushing, coat,
                              collar, mood, Hide Everything (⌘H), Settings (⌘,)

The cat wanders, jumps onto your windows (and rides along if you drag
them), squeezes underneath big windows to hide, naps, grooms, scratches,
gets the zoomies, and pounces at your cursor. If the bowls run empty
they'll sit by them and complain. Their stats only change while your Mac
is awake and Purrgram is running — close the lid and everything pauses.
It's light on battery, and automatically goes easier still in Low Power Mode.

Needs macOS 12 or newer. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
To remove: quit from the menu bar and delete the app.
