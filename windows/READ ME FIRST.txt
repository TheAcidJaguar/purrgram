Purrgram for Windows (beta)
===========================

A little pixel cat that lives on your desktop, walking along the top of your taskbar.

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example Documents).
2. Double-click Purrgram.exe.
   Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't signed
   with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only need to do this once.
3. Your cat appears at the bottom of the screen, next to its food and water bowls.

Playing
- Click the cat to pet it. Double-click to shoo it.
- Drag the cat to pick it up, and let go to drop (or toss) it.
- Click a bowl to fill it.
- Everything else is in the cat icon in the system tray (bottom-right, near the clock;
  it may be hidden under the ^ arrow): stats, fill bowls, coat, mood, rename,
  Start with Windows, and Quit.

The cat hides itself while a full-screen game, video or presentation is running.
Its needs only change while your PC is awake and Purrgram is running.

Needs Windows 10 or 11.
