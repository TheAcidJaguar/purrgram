How to install

Mac

  1. Open Purrgram.dmg and drag Purrgram onto Applications.
  2. Open Purrgram from Applications. macOS says it couldn't verify it, because it's not from the App Store. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Name your cat!

You only do steps 2 and 3 once.

Windows (beta)

  1. Right-click Purrgram-Windows.zip → Extract All, and keep the folder somewhere like Documents.
  2. Double-click Purrgram.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Your cat appears on the taskbar. Its menu is the cat icon near the clock (it may be under the ^ arrow).

The Windows beta keeps the cat on the taskbar, with no toys or sounds yet.

Questions

Why does my computer warn me when I first open it? Is it safe?

Purrgram isn't sold through an app store and isn't registered with Apple or Microsoft (that takes a paid developer account), so your computer asks you to confirm once that you trust it.

The app has no network code at all, so it can't send anything anywhere, and it doesn't ask for any permissions.

Will it slow down my computer?

No. On a Mac it averages about 3% of one processor core. It redraws less often while the cat is calm, pauses completely when your screen sleeps or the lid is closed, and saves extra battery in Low Power Mode. On Windows it also hides during full-screen games, videos and presentations.

Does my cat keep its progress when I update?

Yes. Download the new version and replace the old one. Your cat's name, coat and stats are saved separately, so it carries over.

How do I uninstall it?

Mac: turn off Open at Login in the cat's menu if it's on, choose Quit, and drag Purrgram from Applications to the Trash. To also remove your cat's save, delete ~/Library/Application Support/DesktopCat.

Windows: untick Start with Windows in the tray menu if it's on, choose Quit, and delete the Purrgram folder. Your cat's save is in %APPDATA%\Purrgram.

Does it work with more than one screen?

The cat lives on your main screen (on Windows, along the main screen's taskbar).

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no in-app purchases.
