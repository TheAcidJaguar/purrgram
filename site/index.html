How to install

Mac

  1. Open Purrgram.dmg and drag Purrgram onto Applications.
  2. Open Purrgram from Applications. macOS says it couldn't verify it (it's not from the App Store). Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Name your cat!

You only do steps 2–3 once.

Windows (beta)

  1. Right-click Purrgram-Windows.zip → Extract All, and keep the folder somewhere like Documents.
  2. Double-click Purrgram.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Your cat appears on top of the taskbar. Its menu is the cat icon in the system tray, near the clock (it may be under the ^ arrow).

The Windows version is a lighter beta: the cat stays on the taskbar, and there are no toys or sounds yet.

Questions

Why does my Mac warn me when I first open it? Is it safe?

Purrgram isn't sold through the App Store, and it isn't registered with Apple (that takes a yearly paid developer account), so macOS asks you to confirm once that you trust it. Windows shows a similar "protected your PC" message for the same reason.

The app has no network code at all, so it can't send anything anywhere, and it doesn't ask for any permissions.

Will it slow down my computer?

No. On a Mac it averages about 3% of one processor core. It redraws less often while the cat is calm, pauses completely when your screen sleeps or the lid is closed, and saves even more battery in Low Power Mode. On Windows it hides itself during full-screen games, videos and presentations.

Does my cat keep its progress when I update?

Yes. To update, download the new version and replace the old one. Your cat's name, coat and stats are saved separately, so it carries over.

How do I uninstall it?

Mac: if you turned on Open at Login, turn it off in the cat's menu first. Then choose Quit from the cat face in the menu bar and drag Purrgram from Applications to the Trash. To also remove your cat's save, delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/DesktopCat.

Windows: in the tray menu, untick Start with Windows if it's on, then choose Quit and delete the Purrgram folder. Your cat's save is in %APPDATA%\Purrgram.

Does it work with more than one screen?

The cat lives on your main screen (on Windows, along the main screen's taskbar).

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no in-app purchases.
